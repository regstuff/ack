# The Miraculous Conch - 1. The Miraculous Conch

Once upon a time, there was a poor but generous man whose only companions were a cat and a dog. 

One evening, a weary traveller came to his door. Though he had very little, the generous man gave his guest all the rice he had cooked, meaning he, his cat, and his dog went hungry that night. 

The next morning, the grateful traveller gave the man a beautiful conch shell. "Drop that conch into the pot the next time you cook rice," the traveller instructed before leaving. 

That afternoon, the man boiled a small handful of borrowed rice and dropped the conch into the pot. Immediately, a deliciously appetizing aroma filled the air. 

Drawn by the wonderful smell, hungry neighbours flocked to his house, asking for a taste. The generous old man didn't have the heart to refuse them, though he worried there wouldn't be enough. 

However, to his absolute astonishment, no matter how much rice he served from the pot, the quantity never decreased! "It must be a magical conch!" he realized joyfully. 

Word spread quickly. The old man began selling the endless supply of delicious rice for a small price, and soon he became quite wealthy. He, his dog, and his cat grew plump and happy, never having to worry about starving again. 

But one day, disaster struck. After a group of travellers bought a large quantity of rice, the old man noticed the pot was emptying. He looked inside and gasped— the magical conch was gone! It had accidentally been scooped up and carried away with the travellers' rice. 

The old man searched frantically but couldn't find them. Without the conch, his money slowly ran out, and soon, they were poor and hungry once again. 

Seeing their master's despair, the loyal dog and cat decided to find the conch. The dog carried the cat across the river to the neighbouring village, where they sniffed out the familiar aroma of the magical rice. 

They traced the smell to a locked wooden trunk in a house. Working together, the cat caught a rat and forced it to gnaw a hole in the trunk. The rat pushed the conch out, and the dog grabbed it in his mouth just as the homeowners chased them away. 

Triumphant, they rushed back to the river. The cat climbed onto the dog's back, holding the precious conch securely in her mouth while the dog swam. 

As they crossed, some children on the bank pointed at them and laughed at the funny sight. Hearing them, the foolish cat couldn't help but laugh too. "Hee hee..." 

*PLOP!* The conch fell from her open mouth and vanished into the deep river. 

The furious dog dove after it, leaving the poor cat struggling in the water. She barely managed to paddle to shore and, terrified of the dog's anger, hid in a tree. The dog searched for hours but couldn't retrieve the conch. Defeated, he returned home alone to his master. 

Several hungry days passed. The old man went to the river to beg for food, and a kind fisherman gave him a large, magnificent fish. 

The old man took it home, but as he cut the fish open to clean it, he found something hard inside. "A CONCH!" he cried out in joy. It was his magical conch, swallowed by the fish! 

He immediately started cooking his magical, delicious rice again. The wonderful aroma drifted up to the tree where the cat was hiding. Unable to resist the smell of the food she loved, she sheepishly returned home. 

"Welcome home!" the old man greeted her warmly. And from that day on, the old man, his dog, and his cat lived happily and comfortably, never going hungry again.


# The Miraculous Conch - 2. A Game of Chess

Once upon a time, there was a despotic king who had a very strange and cruel method of acquiring slaves. Anyone who came to him asking for a favour or permission had to first play a game of chess with him. 

But it wasn't a normal game. The King had a highly trained pet cat. 

"It's not your skill, but this cat's that will decide the outcome," the King would explain. Seven lit lamps would be carefully balanced on the cat's tail. "If she moves and drops even one of the lamps before the game ends, I shall grant whatever you request. But if she sits perfectly still until the end of the game, you shall become my slave for life!"

Unfortunately for everyone who played, the cat was incredibly well-trained and terrified of the King. She would sit as still as a statue, and the King never lost. Countless men had been enslaved this way. 

One day, a merchant from a distant city arrived, seeking permission to trade. When the minister explained the strange rule, the merchant, who was an excellent chess player, decided to take the risk. "I have come a long distance and I cannot go back empty-handed," he reasoned. 

He played brilliantly, doing everything possible to prolong the game, hoping the cat would eventually tire and move. But the cat remained completely immobile. Finally, to his horror, the merchant lost the game. 

"I have won. The game is over and the cat has not moved!" the King declared triumphantly. The devastated merchant had to send a message back to his wife, explaining his terrible plight. 

The merchant's wife was a very brave and clever woman. "I must secure his freedom somehow... and teach that wicked king a lesson," she vowed. "How dare he let a cat decide my husband's fate! A cat... wait! That gives me an idea!"

Dressing herself up as a man, the clever wife travelled to the palace with one of her trusted servants. She asked for a favour and readily agreed to the King's bizarre chess game. 

The game began. The King played confidently, assuming he had won another slave. But outside the room, the woman's servant was waiting. Following her instructions, the servant quietly released a small mouse into the playing room. 

The cat immediately stiffened and pricked up her ears, tempted to pounce. But the King glared fiercely at her, and she forced herself to stay still. 

A little later, the servant released a second mouse. The cat moved restlessly, almost dropping a lamp. The King grew desperate and tried to finish the game quickly. "I must be alert. Thank God my husband taught me all the tricky moves!" the disguised wife thought, expertly countering the King's rapid plays. 

Just as the tension peaked, the servant released a third mouse. 

This time, the feline instinct was simply too strong. The cat could not restrain herself. She leaped forward to catch the mouse, violently upsetting all seven lamps from her tail! 

"I HAVE WON!" the woman cheered. "THE CAT HAS MOVED AND THE GAME HAS NOT ENDED!"

The King was furious but trapped by his own rules. "What do you want from me?" he demanded bitterly. 

"I want you to release all the men you have enslaved!" she commanded. 

The defeated King had no choice but to grant her request. The brave and clever woman freed her husband and all the other unfortunate slaves, and they returned home together in triumph.
